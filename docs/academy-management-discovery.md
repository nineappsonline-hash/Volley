# Academy management search and AI discovery

Implemented 14 October 2026.

## Public entry points

- English: `/academy-management`
- Arabic: `/ar/academy-management`
- Homepage: `/#academy-management` (content follows the selected language)
- Existing `/solutions` and sport-specific solution pages link to the management guide.

The pages address academy owners and coaches looking for software, rather than players looking for training. They explain existing member subscriptions, schedules, QR attendance, payment records and session finances, with registration at `/signup/tenant`. No private academy records are exposed and no dashboard permissions change.

## Search intent

Primary English topics: sports academy management software in Egypt, academy management app, player subscription management, sports academy attendance software.

Primary Arabic topics: برنامج إدارة الأكاديميات الرياضية في مصر، نظام إدارة أكاديمية رياضية، إدارة اشتراكات اللاعبين، برنامج حضور الأكاديميات، إدارة مدفوعات الأكاديمية.

These topics appear in visible headings and descriptive copy, not only metadata. Both guides include reciprocal language alternates, self-canonicals, social previews, breadcrumbs, software features and FAQs matching visible content. Sitemap entries and `llms.txt` links help discovery. No unverified price, review score or ranking claim is attached to the new software schema. The old hardcoded offer was removed from sport solution schema.
